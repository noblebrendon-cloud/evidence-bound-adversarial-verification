## Case-study source classification

The Jesus Is King case chronology is grounded in review reports and user-provided summaries in the project conversation. Primary records were only partly accessible to the independent reviewer in several reviews. No complete restricted records are republished here. The paper labels the retrospective case, test totals and remaining gaps accordingly.

## Research limitations

This is a scoping and synthesis review, not a systematic literature review. It uses important primary standards, foundational publications and official documentation. It does not establish historical priority, exhaustive related work, or comparative evaluation of EBAV.
